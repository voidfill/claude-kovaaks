## Difficulty tiers

Four tiers, easiest first: `Easier` · `Medium` · `Hard` · `Expert`.

The benchmark is a fixed set of 39 **slots**; each tier fills every slot with its own
scenario. Usually that is the same scenario family made harder, but not always — slot 9
is `PGTI Voltaic` at three tiers and `VT PreciseOrb` at Medium, and slot 21 is a
`1w6ts/1w5ts reload` variant at three tiers and `1w4ts Voltaic` at Expert.

## Ranks

Viscose rank names are themed per tier rather than forming one ladder, so classify an
unfamiliar rank name by what kind of word it is:

| Rank name looks like | Tier |
|---|---|
| an animal (seal, …) | Easier |
| a colour (fuchsia, …) | Medium |
| a fabric (silk, …) | Hard |
| something grandiose (mythic, heroic, …) | Expert |

Individual rank names were not verified; the theme rule is the reliable part.

## Groups

Four groups, fourteen subgroups. **This vocabulary is Viscose's own** — "Flick Tech" and
"Click Timing" have no Voltaic equivalent, and Viscose's "Control Tracking → Blending" is
the closest thing it has to Voltaic's "Tracking → Precise". See the cross-walk in
`../benchmarks.md`, and confirm with the user rather than substituting.

| Group | Subgroups | Slots |
|---|---|---|
| Control Tracking | Arm, Wrist, Fingertip, Blending | 0-11 |
| Reactive Tracking | Control, Speed, Reading | 12-17 |
| Flick Tech | Speed, Stability, Micro, Post-flick | 18-29 |
| Click Timing | Reading, Precision, Stability | 30-38 |

## Training method

Viscose has no VDIM equivalent — it is trained by running the benchmark scenarios
directly. A subgroup playlist is just that subgroup's scenarios at the chosen tier.
Use play count 1 to measure, 2-3 to train.

For a user below the Easier tier, the index also carries `Viscose Benchmarks - Beginner`
and the `Viscose benchmarks easier/medium/hard scenarios` sets; find them with
`search-playlists` rather than assuming a name.
